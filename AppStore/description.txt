Tilt Trail is a planned offline iPhone tilt-labyrinth game. Guide a tactile marble through original, handcrafted obstacle courses with touch drag or optional calibrated device tilt.

Features planned for development:
- Handcrafted courses with hazards, clear checkpoints, and finish lines.
- Choice between direct touch steering and opt-in device tilt with neutral calibration.
- Local time trials and personal bests stored entirely on your device.
- Transparent checkpoint restarts so you can master difficult segments.
- Private by design: no accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking, and no ads.
- JSON export and import for full ownership of your records.

Tilt Trail is built specifically for iPhone in native Swift.
