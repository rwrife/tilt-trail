Tilt Trail is an offline iPhone tilt-labyrinth game. Guide a marble through three original obstacle courses with touch drag or optional calibrated device tilt.

- Navigate walls, pits, checkpoints, and finish lines in Switchback, Sundial, and Three Bends.
- Use touch steering by default. Enable tilt in settings and calibrate a neutral angle when device motion is available.
- Retry from the start or the last checkpoint; pause and resume a run.
- Beat your personal best time on each course, saved locally on your device.
- Export local times and tilt preferences as JSON, validate imports before replacing data, or delete all local data after confirmation.

No accounts, cloud sync, tracking, ads, or network features. Tilt Trail is built for iPhone in native Swift.
